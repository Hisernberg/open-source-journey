## How to pick your 3 primaries (by ~Feb 21, 2027)

1. Overlap: org must contain ≥ 2 repos from your domain portfolios (see the other target files).
2. Evidence: you already have 5+ merged PRs (or easy paths to them) in that org's repos.
3. Accessibility: active maintainer response time < 7 days; clear idea list published.
4. Project fit: at least one idea page you could genuinely own end-to-end.

## Proposal hygiene (for Mar 18 – Apr 2, 2027 window)

- Read the org's past accepted proposals (many orgs publish them) — mirror structure and depth.
- Talk to mentors **before** writing; a proposal nobody expects loses.
- Include: deliverables per week, stretch goals, CI/testing plan, personal contribution evidence.
